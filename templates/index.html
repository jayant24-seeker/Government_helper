About CitizenConnect

Citizens can report potholes, garbage overflow and broken streetlights with location and photo evidence. Staff can claim complaints, update status and attach resolution proof.

How it works

1. Register

Create your citizen account using your phone number and password. No OTP.

2. Report

Submit an issue with a description, pincode, GPS location and optional photo.

3. Track

Use your permanent Track ID to see live complaint status and updates.
